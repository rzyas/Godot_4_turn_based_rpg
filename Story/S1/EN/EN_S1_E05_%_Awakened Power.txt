The wolves' growls grew closer. Their glowing eyes glittered in the darkness, encircling Karin, Reza, and Dimas. Their breaths came in gasps, their bodies trembled, but from somewhere, a strange instinct emerged within them.

Suddenly, in the midst of their fear, courage surged.

Karin gripped the air—and in her hand, an golden sword appeared, softly glowing but as sharp as captured sunlight. Reza looked at his own hand, and a magic staff appeared, its tip burning with a blue fire that spun like a star. Dimas, who had been touched by a mysterious shadow from the beginning, now held a black spear, a thick aura flowing from it, stronger than ever.

The primitive weapons they had relied on—sharp sticks, rocks, a small knife—fell to the ground, meaningless.

The wolves lunged. But this time, the three of them were no longer prey. Karin screamed, swinging her golden sword, her strike illuminating the air. Reza raised his staff, a ball of blue fire shot out and exploded in front of the wolves, making the ground tremble. Dimas, with a shout of anger, plunged his black spear into the ground, causing shadows to crawl like roots and restrain the enemies' steps.

The fight... was short. Too short.

The once-fearsome wolves fell one by one. Some were burned, some were slashed, and others were impaled by black shadows. Their breaths were ragged, their bodies sweaty, but the only injuries they had were minor scratches.

It was not a normal victory. It was a foreign power, something that was not theirs, but now flowed through every nerve in their bodies.

When silence returned, they looked at each other. Adrenaline was still pumping, but what emerged was not relief—but confusion and unease.

"This weapon..." Karin stared at her sword, her fingers trembling.

"Why... why can we all do this now?" asked Reza, still gasping for air.

Dimas clutched his spear tightly, his face tense. "Before, it was only me... now it's all three of us. It's like something... wants us to be strong."

A moment of silence. Only the small blue fire on Reza's staff glowed faintly.

Finally, Karin said in a soft but firm voice:

"No matter where it comes from... if this can help us survive, then we must accept it."

Reza nodded heavily. Dimas looked down, not entirely in agreement, but knowing they had no choice.

They folded the worn-out map they had found, staring at the distant markers. The journey was still long, deeper, more dangerous. They agreed to stop at several points, making each location a small hope for survival, before reaching the mysterious "X".

Their next step led them to a place different from the forest and the dead village: an ancient temple half-buried in moss and tree roots. Its walls had collapsed, the statues were cracked, yet a thick ancient aura still remained.

But the temple was not silent. From the dark crevices, giant insects began to crawl out, their eyes glowing, their wings trembling, a humming sound piercing their ears.

Karin raised her sword. Reza gripped his staff. Dimas plunged his spear into the ground.

That power rose again, pumping a spirit they thought was gone.

The next battle awaited.
