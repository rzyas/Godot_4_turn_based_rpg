The traps they set in the dead village finally worked. As night fell, the sound of a snapped twig was heard, and a graceful deer with softly glowing antlers walked into their simple rope trap. The three children were shocked, their eyes wide, their hearts pounding.

"It's... the legendary deer!" Karin whispered, her voice trembling with a mix of awe and relief.

Reza held his breath, as if afraid the slightest movement would scare the creature away. Dimas was even close to tears.

They rushed forward. The deer struggled, its eyes reflecting a soft, soothing, yet mysterious light. They were certain this was the creature from the stories, the reason they had dared to enter the Forbidden Forest.

"Finally..." Reza muttered, his voice hoarse. "Finally, all of this wasn't for nothing."

But it wasn't just the deer that was caught. Several other traps had also worked: a large rabbit was snared, and even a jungle fowl was caught. That night, they cooked the rabbit meat, sharing mouthfuls while watching the deer from a distance. They refused to touch the deer yet. Karin even whispered to Reza and Dimas, "Tomorrow morning... we will ask it. Maybe we can go home. Or at least... be safe."

That night, they fell asleep with a glimmer of hope, though the surrounding forest was still noisy with strange sounds, indistinct screams from a distance, as if the world itself was laughing at them.

Morning came. The sun's faint light filtered through the trees. The deer was still there, still trapped. But what they saw broke their hearts. The deer hadn't changed. It didn't speak. It didn't shine brighter. It couldn't even free itself from the rope.

The deer was just an ordinary animal.

Silence. Karin stood stiffly, her eyes welling up. Dimas hit the ground, his face red with anger. Reza could only stare at the deer for a long time, speechless. Their hope collapsed instantly. All the stories, all the tales... were just lies.

With heavy hearts, they finally did the unimaginable. They killed the deer, cutting its body into provisions. Karin's tears fell freely as her hands became stained with blood. Dimas covered his face, sobbing. Reza held back his emotions, though inside he was shattered. Their last hope had now turned into salted meat, wrapped crudely.

That day, they decided not just to survive, but to explore even further. If the story of the deer was just a lie, perhaps there was another truth in this forest.

In one of the dilapidated houses in the dead village, they found something strange: a worn-out map. On it, there were location markers, with one "X" more prominent than the others. The location was not far from the dead village, just a few days' journey away.

They looked at each other. There was fear, but also a drive to keep going.

The journey began. Their footsteps were heavy, their bodies still weak, but a new determination had begun to grow. They followed the map's direction, passing through dense trees and winding paths. Every sound of a snapping twig made them cautious, every dark shadow made their hearts beat faster.

Until finally, they arrived at a low-lying area, not far from the "X" on the map. There, from behind a bush, a low sound was heard—a long growl that made their hair stand on end.

From the darkness, several pairs of glowing eyes emerged. Large wolves, with matted fur and blood-stained teeth, blocked their path.

Karin gripped a sharp stick. Reza raised a rock in his hand. Dimas, though his body was trembling, felt a shadowy energy rise within him again.

They had no other choice. Once again, they had to fight.
