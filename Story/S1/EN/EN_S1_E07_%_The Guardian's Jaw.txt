Morning arrived. The sun slipped faintly through the cracks of the trees, yet its light could not banish the heavy haze that clung to the air.
Empty stomachs, trembling bodies, their last scraps of food long gone. Today was no longer about exploring, but surviving with what little strength remained.

Their hearts pounded faster as their feet slowly descended from the temple’s peak.
The lower they went, the stronger the aura became. An unseen pressure crushed against their chests, choking their breath.

A growl echoed—closer this time. Leaves trembled.
Glowing red eyes pierced through the darkness of the trees.

And finally… it appeared.

A monstrous wolf with antlers like a stag. Its fur was pitch black, its body towering like a house, its fangs glistening wet, and its gaze radiating something beyond hunger—ancient hatred. Every step shook the earth, every breath made the air heavier.

Karin, Reza, and Dimas stood firm. Their mysterious weapons manifested at once: a golden sword, a staff of blue fire, and a black spear. Three small lights against the looming darkness.

The battle began.

The beast lunged with impossible speed for a body so massive. Karin slashed the air with her golden sword, its flash of light colliding with claws the size of tree trunks. The impact cracked the earth. Reza raised his staff high, summoning blue fire that erupted like a storm, scorching patches of the monster’s fur.

But the attack only enraged it.

Its roar shook the air, pressing against their ears until they nearly burst.
Dimas charged forward with his black spear, shadows spreading across the ground, trying to bind the monster’s colossal legs. For a moment, the beast faltered—then its antlers swept sideways, striking Reza.

Reza’s body was hurled into a rock, blood spraying from his mouth. His staff slipped from his hand, the blue fire snuffed out. He collapsed, motionless.

“REZA!!” Karin screamed, eyes brimming with tears—but there was no time. The monster lunged again. Karin blocked with her golden blade, while Dimas drove his spear into the beast’s flank, black blood spraying out. The creature roared in agony, yet it still stood strong.

They knew… this was not a fight they could win.

With the last of his strength, Dimas dashed to Reza, hoisting him onto his back. Karin struck with one final golden slash to force the monster back, then shouted:
“RUN!!”

They sprinted with everything they had. The ground trembled behind them, colossal claws slicing the air. A chasm opened before them. No choice remained.

With a desperate cry, they leapt. Their bodies plunged into the darkness, then crashed into the raging river below.

The water greeted them with a deathly chill. The current dragged them apart, slamming them against rocks, spinning them in whirlpools. Reza lay unconscious on Dimas’ back, blood flowing endlessly. Karin fought to keep him from sinking, but her strength was fading.

From above the cliff, the antlered beast stood at the edge. Its red eyes glared down, filled with triumph, a gaze that said: “You are nothing but prey.”

The river pulled them onward. Hungry, exhausted, broken.
Karin’s vision dimmed. Dimas staggered, still swimming, but the world spun around him.

Everything slowly dissolved into darkness.

The roar of the river was the last sound they heard.
