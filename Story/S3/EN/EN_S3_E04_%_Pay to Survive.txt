[SCENE: AMONG SUSPICION]
The wind bit their skin as they walked across the seemingly endless snowfield.
The rough map the survivors had given them remained folded in Reza's hands.
He stared intently at it, then said softly:
‘What if this is a trap? They could be waiting for us there… plundering us when we're off guard.’

Dimas sighed,
‘I thought that too… But we have no other choice.’

They stood for a long time on the icy ridge, staring out at the empty horizon of bluish white.
A decision was made: they wouldn't head straight for the point on the map.
They would circle the main route, surveying from afar, waiting for signs of life… before approaching.

[SCENE: DEtour]
Three days became five.
Five became ten.
The snow continued to fall mercilessly.
Supplies dwindled, and our steps grew heavier.
Each night they slept in a small, frozen tent, taking turns keeping watch to prevent any nocturnal creatures from arriving.

It had now been almost two weeks since they left the ruins.
And finally… signs of life appeared.

[SCENE: FIRST SIGHT]
A faint smoke in the distance.
Then high walls of ice blocks and scrap metal, topped with sharpened ice spears.
The sound of people, faint but distinct, pierced the gentle storm.

Reza and Dimas stopped behind a large snowdrift.
Reza stared at the fortress for a long moment, then smiled wryly:
‘It seems… maybe the survivors are good people after all.’

Atop the defense tower, several heavily robed guards armed with bows stood frozen like statues, their sharp eyes scanning the surroundings.

[SCENE: A GATE THAT'S NOT CHEAP]
As they approached the main gate, a guard dismounted and called out loudly:
‘To enter, you must deposit mana stones as collateral! No exceptions!’

Reza reached into his leather pouch… there were only a few small pieces.
It was far from enough.

[SCENE: OLD SURVIVORS’ HUMOR]
From a distance, some of the survivors they had met before happened to pass by, waving and laughing.
One of them shouted:
‘Damn! We forgot to tell you we had to pay tribute to get in!’

Another added with a laugh,
‘But you’re not weaklings, are you? You can definitely hunt a few small monsters first!’

[SCENE: THEIR DETERMINATION]
Reza just shrugged.
Dimas straightened his spear, a faint smile forming on his chapped lips.
‘No problem,’ he said quietly.
‘In the end… survival is the only way.’

They both turned, preparing to re-enter the frozen snowfield—
to hunt.
