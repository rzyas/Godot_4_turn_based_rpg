[SCENE: PORTAL TRANSITION]
The purple light from the portal slowly faded, turning to grayish white. Reza and Dimas slammed onto the hard, snowy surface. An instant later, the portal behind them closed with a soft, but deadly, click.
Behind the fading vortex, the cold—evil—smirks of the royal magicians grinned, as if savoring the tragedy that was about to befall these two young men.

[SCENE: VOID]
Silence.
Complete silence.
Not just any silence, but a silence that swallowed sound. The wind blew the dry snow, but nothing could be heard, as if the world denied the presence of sound.
Reza opened his mouth, trying to speak, but only a puff of steam escaped. No sound. No echo. Dimas tried to scream… still empty.

Then it came—a bone-chilling cold,
seeping through the gaps in their clothes, creeping into their joints, tearing at their lungs from within. Their bodies rejected the reality of the temperature that had suddenly plunged them from the desert to the poles in an instant.

[SCENE: COLLAPSE SYMPTOMS]
Reza sank to his knees, his hands shaking uncontrollably. Dimas tried to reach for him, but his fingers were numb. Their breaths were ragged, but each breath only sent ice into their lungs. Reza's eyes watered, not from emotion, but because his eyeballs were frozen at the edges.

Their bodies were shocked by the extreme temperature difference.
The human body wasn't made for a jump like this.
Muscles tensed, blood vessels constricted drastically, and hearts beat faster—nearly collapsing.

Dimas wrote in the snow with frozen fingers:
'CLI…F…'

Reza nodded weakly. They knew: if they didn't take shelter within 10 minutes, they would freeze… forever.

[SCENE: CRAWLING JOURNEY]
Two small silhouettes crawled through the storm. Each step seemed to drag their bodies toward the abyss of death. The snow continued to swallow their tracks, as if trying to erase their existence from the world.
Reza's fingers began to turn blue, Dimas gritted his frozen teeth. There was no sound. Even the crunch of snow beneath their feet was lost in the freezing air.

Then—
in the distance, darkness.
A black cavity in the icy cliff. A cave.

[SCENE: SNOW CAVE]
They crawled inside. The walls were cold but kept the wind out. They leaned against each other, shivering uncontrollably. Reza opened his bag and took out their last tiny shard of blue mana crystal—a tiny shard from the second floor. His hand could barely hold it.

He pressed it against his staff, forcing a small spark.
Tap.
The blue flame flickered, then flared softly—
and the first sound after a long silence was finally heard: the hiss of fire.

Dimas stared at the flame as if it were the light of life. Their eyes filled with tears not from sadness—but from relief to be alive.

[SCENE: REZA'S MONOLOGUE]
Reza stared at the flames and whispered hoarsely:
‘We… can’t die here… Karin… hasn’t been reciprocated…’

[SCENE: CHANGE]
But this world doesn’t give you a break.
Beyond the cave wall, a heavy rumbling could be heard—
Not wind.
Not snow.
Footsteps.

A giant shadow passed through the cave entrance.
A body as big as a house, with white fur and glowing red eyes in the darkness.
A beast… or perhaps something more ancient than this world.

Reza and Dimas stared at each other. Their breathing was starting to return, but their bodies were still half-dead.
But they knew one thing:

If they didn't stand up now… they would die on the spot.
