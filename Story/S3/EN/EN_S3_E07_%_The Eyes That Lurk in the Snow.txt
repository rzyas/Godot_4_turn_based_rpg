The morning air bit at their skin, but their steps felt lighter.
Dimas carried a sack full of dark blue mana stone fragments—the result of yesterday's brutal battle in the frozen ruins. Reza walked beside him, his eyes constantly scanning the massive defensive wall looming before them.

As they approached, two snow-armed guards lowered their ice spears, eyeing the sack in Dimas's hands.
One of them whistled softly.
"Where did you get so many... and still be alive?"

Dimas just smiled faintly. "From a place we shouldn't have come."

[SCENE: A LOOK BEHIND THE WALL]

The iron gate opened slowly, its heavy sound echoing. As soon as they stepped inside, the world changed—
An open-air market stretched beyond the wall: thick cloth tents, large bonfires, vendors selling silver-plated weapons, storm-proof robes, blood-red potions, and delicious-looking smoked food.

And suddenly… all eyes turned toward them.

Stares of awe.
Stares of wonder.
And… sly looks.

Whispers began to flow through the crowd:

“That’s a fragment of the Frost Titan’s core…”
“Two kids… killed that giant?”
“If they could do it… imagine the mana stone they were carrying…”

Reza gripped his staff tightly, his jaw clenched.
“I think we just announced to the world that we’re rich,” he muttered.

[SCENE: GOOD GUARD]

A friendlier-looking gatekeeper approached, his face shadowed behind a thick hood.
He patted Dimas’s shoulder gently, then spoke softly:

“From now on… trust no one.
That mana stone not only attracts admiration… but also kills.”

Dimas and Reza glanced at each other, nodding slowly. The words hung like a cold mist between them.

[SCENE: BIG SHOPPING]

They headed straight for the main market center.
Shining weapons, lightweight armor made of snow steel, instant healing potions, and dried food that would last for months—everything they had once only dreamed of was now in their hands.

Reza could hardly believe it as he handed over a single mana stone fragment.
The merchant weighed it, then let out a short laugh.

“Even after buying all this… you’ve only spent half its value.”

Dimas turned in surprise.
“Half…?!”
The kind guard, who had been watching from afar, walked over and added:

“If you only buy food and basic supplies…
…with the remaining mana stones, you can last three years.”

The sound made them freeze for a moment. Three years.
They stared at each other—their eyes both sparkling… and anxious.

[SCENE: SHADOWS IN THE STORM]

That night a blizzard raged, battering their tent like a thousand icy whips.
They sat propped up against a boulder, wrapped in their new, thick blankets, staring at the small fire that was struggling to stay alive.

The wind howled like wolves from the sky.
Dimas chuckled, trying to dispel the tension.
"Funny, huh... yesterday we almost died. Today we look like royalty."

Reza nodded slowly, his eyes heavy with sleep. But just as they were about to close... he saw something in the distance.

Black shadows.
Walking upright through the storm, their robes fluttering like shreds of night. There were three... five... seven figures—and none of them were affected by the fierce wind or the blinding snow.

Their magical aura was so dense that the storm around them parted.

Dimas slowly reached for his spear.
Reza stared intently, his breath frozen in the air.
There was no sound except the roar of the storm... and the approaching footsteps of the figures.
