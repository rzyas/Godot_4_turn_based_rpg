The arctic wind roared like thousands of blades of ice grinding against each other. Atop a frozen rock cliff, two silhouettes stood: Reza and Dimas.

Their bodies trembled… not from fear, but from the bone-chilling cold that seemed to freeze even their blood. Old wounds blackened their skin, covered by thick layers of armor with magical crystals glowing faintly between them.

A few hours ago, they had nearly died. A black-scaled monster emerging from a crack in the ice had nearly crushed them—and that was when they realized:

“Strength… isn’t everything,” Reza muttered under his breath.
“Without a plan… we’re just frozen meat waiting to be eaten,” Dimas replied, his eyes staring blankly into the abyss below.

For the first time in a long time, they felt truly vulnerable. They realized: strength, crystals, and magic mean nothing without preparation, planning, information, and experience.

[SCENE: CHASED AND SLAUGHTERED]

After they decided to separate from the other survivors, news of the “titan monster conqueror” spread like wildfire.

Several large groups hunted them, hoping to seize their treasures and weapons.

Other groups offered cooperation, promising warmth and protection.

But they refused them all.
They trusted only each other.
Naive… but it was the only thing keeping them alive.

“If we join forces, we’ll just be pawns in their game,” Reza had said at the time.
“It’s better to die together… than to live as their tools,” Dimas replied.

Ironically, the reason they remained safe was precisely because everyone feared them.
A terrifying legend had formed by word of mouth:

‘Two young men… who killed a Titan with their bare hands.’

No one dared to get too close.

[SCENE: FREEZING WOUNDS]

Four years had passed.
The snow never stopped.
Their bodies were now covered in scars, their hands rough from cold and war, but their eyes burned with experience.

Three years ago, they heard a thunderous news:
The fellow survivor who had given them the black crystal had slain the Legendary Ice Dragon and ascended to the next floor.

From that day on… they knew where their journey would end.

But silently, the memories of their childhood home began to slowly fade.
The green grass, the village streets, the afternoon light through the wooden windows… everything was like shadows slowly freezing in their hearts.
They no longer even remembered their parents' voices.

“Funny, isn't it… I even forgot my mother's face,” Reza whispered softly.
“That means we've been here too long,” Dimas replied, looking up at the gray sky.

[SCENE: BATTLE DAY]

Today… they were no longer the little children shivering under the tree. They were among the strongest survivors to ever set foot on the 3rd Floor.
Shining armor of enchanted metal, weapons with crystalline blades gleaming blue and black, and eyes that had seen hundreds of deaths.

And now—
Before them, the air froze.

The snow stopped falling.

The sky split with a low roar.

From behind the white clouds, a giant, winged figure emerged…
Its wings were as large as mountains, its scales gleaming like frozen mirrors.
Its breath froze the air into shards of glass.

It was…
The Legendary Ice Dragon.

“Four years…” Dimas whispered, gripping his black spear.
“Four years just to stand here,” Reza replied, raising his golden sword glowing blue.

Their gazes froze—
sharp, burning, piercing the blizzard.

And with one simultaneous shout—

THEY JUMPED TOWARDS THE DRAGON.

Their swords and spears flashed, leaving a trail of light in the frozen sky.
Their screams tore through thousands of meters of icy air.

The battle began.
