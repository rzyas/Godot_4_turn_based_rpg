Snow fell slowly like ash from the gray sky.
Reza woke first, finding the small fire they had built completely extinguished. His breath drifted out like thin smoke in the freezing air. Dimas was still asleep, his face peaceful—but his lips were blue.

Reza took a deep breath and patted Dimas on the shoulder.
“Wake up. We have to find more mana stones… before we turn to ice.”

Dimas opened his eyes slowly and nodded.
They were both running out of food. Their mana stones were barely enough to cover another night of shelter. They had only one option: hunting.

[SCENE: FOOTPRINTS IN THE SNOW]

They walked through the light storm, following a small, barely visible path, only vaguely marked by the traders they had encountered yesterday:

“To the east… there are the ruins of a frozen city. But be careful. No one ever comes back from there intact.”

Their footsteps made the crunchy sound of cracking snow.
The air felt oppressive, quiet… too quiet. Until suddenly, Dimas stopped.

“Did you hear that?”
“Hear what?” Reza asked.

A sound like a twig snapping… then it was gone.
They stared at each other. There were no trees around, just a vast white plain. Reza gripped his cane tightly.

Then the ground beneath them cracked.

[SCENE: FROZEN CITY RUINS]

They fell down, hitting the snow hard, rolling over the icy rocks.
When the snow dust settled… their eyes widened.

A giant city frozen underground.
Buildings towered overhead, covered in ice. The streets cracked. Humanoid statues froze in running positions—as if the city had frozen in a single second of destruction.

“Dimas… this—”
“Don’t tell me this place is empty.”

And it wasn’t empty.

From the shadows, slender creatures like decaying snowmen emerged, their eyes glowing blue. They moved like living ice cracks. Each step they took created a new crack in the ground.

[SCENE: BATTLE IN THE FROZEN CITY]

Dimas immediately threw his spear, shattering one creature into shards. But two others ambushed him from the side.
Reza leaped onto the rubble, shooting blue fire magic from his staff—fire that immediately dimmed upon contact with the cold air.

“They’re absorbing heat!!” Reza shouted.
“Good, then we’ve made them full!”

Dimas grinned, then stabbed his spear into the ground, releasing heat energy from his reserve mana stones. The explosion of steam blinded his vision—enough to freeze the creatures in place, then crack simultaneously like glass.

Silence.
Their breaths were ragged. Dimas fell to his knees, his chest heaving rapidly.

In the center of the city square, they found a large, dark blue mana stone embedded in the chest of a giant statue. As Reza approached…

The statue moved.

[SCENE: ICE GIANT]

A cracking sound thundered. The giant statue slowly turned its head, the ice covering it falling away piece by piece.

Its eyes flashed a blinding white.

Reza and Dimas were instantly thrown backward as the giant's hand slammed into the ground. A wave of ice spread like shards of glass hurtling toward them.

“RUN!”
They jumped from rooftop to rooftop, chased by the giant ice arm that sliced ​​through buildings like paper. The mana stone glowed brighter with each movement, like its own heart.

Dimas stabbed his spear into the wall, swung, and then threw it at the giant's feet, instantly stopping the monster.
“Reza, now!!”

Reza gathered all his blue fire mana, his eyes glowing, and fired it straight into the statue's chest—the giant's mana stone.

The explosion silenced the entire city.

Silence After the Explosion

When the ice dust settled, the giant had shattered into dimly glowing shards.
The large mana stone had now shattered into several medium-sized pieces—enough to cover several weeks of equipment.

Reza lay in the snow, his chest heaving.
Dimas sat against the rubble, chuckling through chapped lips.
“I hate this place… but at least we’re alive.”

They looked at each other, then at the mana stone shard.
Somehow, for the first time since entering the 3rd floor… they felt a little triumphant.
