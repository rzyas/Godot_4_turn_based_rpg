Dimas fell to the ground, his body half frozen, half bleeding.
The remnants of battle floated in the cold air: shards of bone, shattered ice, and the pale blue glow of the Legendary Ice Dragon they had just defeated.

Or… the one Dimas thought had fallen.

Before him, the giant heart beat slowly… then faster, louder, stronger.
Dimas stared at it with half-open eyes, his breath frozen in the air.

“This… isn’t a portal to the next floor…”
his voice hoarse, almost drowned in the rumbling.

[SCENE: RETURN TO ICE HELL]

Not a new world.
Not hope.
The heart didn’t drag him to the next floor…

…but returned him to the dragon’s frozen dimension.

The sky cracked again, splitting into a chasm of ice.
Islands of snow floated in the void.
Around him, thousands of skeletons greeted his return—silent, frozen, and dead.

The dragon’s body began to disintegrate.
Its wings shattered, its tail crumbled into icy dust, its entire body drifted toward its heart.

One by one, they reintegrated into the cold flesh—
rebuilding the monster.

Dimas tried to lift his body, but only blood came from his mouth.

“Damn… I can’t… I can’t…”

[SCENE: FINAL SECONDS]

His vision began to fade.
It was like the world was turning in slow motion, like time was refusing to move.

Dimas knew: this was the end.
He wouldn’t get out of here.
There would be no miracle. No savior.

No Reza.
No Karin.
Just him… and death.

But, on the edge between life and death, he thought:
If I can’t kill this dragon, at least I can make sure someone else will one day…

His hands trembled, but he lifted a black mana crystal.
He poured all his energy, all his soul, all his life… into the crystal.

His magic formed a complex magical code —
information about all the dragon's weaknesses:
the gap in its neck bones, its frozen breathing pattern, its heartbeat rhythm when opening a portal.

The crystal vibrated, glowing red, then froze pitch black.
The Mana Code had been born.

With his last breath, Dimas threw it.
The crystal shot like a shooting star, piercing through the layers of dimensions, disappearing into the distance…
waiting for someone powerful enough and crazy enough to read it someday.

[SCENE: THE ICE KING'S VICTORY]

And then it happened…

The Legendary Ice Dragon fully regenerated.
Its flesh grew back, its scales fused together, its eyes opened through the mist.

It stood—larger, more whole, more vicious than ever.
Its roar exploded the entire icy dimension.

RAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARGHHH!
Instantly, everything became a plain of ice before the battle began, leaving only the remaining souls: the Ice Dragon and Dimas on the brink of death.

The ice island shattered, the storm raged again, and snow fell like glass knives from the sky.

Dimas could barely lift his head.
His left hand was the only thing left.

The dragon slowly crept closer…
Every step made the world tremble, making space seem to crack.

Its giant claw rose…
It wanted to end it all.

Dimas stared at it, blood dripping from his chin, then raised his middle finger.
The large, terrifying, clawed hand approached quickly.

“Damn you…”

[SCENE: DARKNESS]

The claw descended.
The world shattered.
Ice exploded in all directions.

Then—silence.
Darkness.

No feeling.
No cold.
No sound.

[SCENE: RETURNING MEMORIES]

…Then…
…from a distance…
a small voice was heard.

“Dimas…”

a soft voice…
a girl’s voice.

The voice was broken like an echo in a dream.

“Let’s play…”
“Reza’s waiting…”
“This way…”

A warm light emerged from a gap in the darkness.
Not magic light.
Sunlight.

Dimas opened his eyes—
And he saw green grass.
Wildflowers.
Blue sky.

He was standing in a small meadow outside their former small village.
Warm air.
A gentle breeze.

And in the distance, Karin ran, waving, her smile as wide as the sky.
Reza followed behind, laughing like they had before they knew the meaning of death.

“Dimas! Hurry, let’s go to the hill!”
“You’re so slow!”

Dimas fell silent.
Tears fell down his cheeks.
He looked back…
There was no storm. No blood. No dragon.

Just the world… as it was before everything was destroyed.

His steps were unsteady, but he walked toward them.
Closer.
Warmer.

And as the sunlight enveloped his vision…

He smiled.

The journey ends where it all began.
No more fighting.
No more crying.
Just little children…
…and the light that waits beyond the snow.

— The end.
