[SCENE: SUSPICIOUS SILENCE]
Reza held his breath. Dimas gripped his spear tightly.
“Who’s outside?!’ Reza shouted, his voice echoing softly in the cave.

The rock at the entrance moved for a moment…
then there was silence.

The small, white-furred creature that had slept nearby the night before suddenly panicked—
it clawed at the ground, dug with astonishing speed, and disappeared beneath.
Reza’s eyes widened.
Around them, several other pairs of tiny eyes poked out from crevices in the rocks: there were many other small creatures—all digging downward, disappearing into the ground as if they knew something they didn’t.

[SCENE: A THREAT EMBRACES]
Dimas whispered:
“If they’re running away… it means there’s danger.”

Suddenly… the hand reappeared.
But it wasn’t a human hand.

Its skin was jet black, cracked like charcoal, its nails long and dug into the stone. The hands clawed in, rolling away the boulders as if they were paper.

Then the figure came into view:
a human-like body, but hunched, bones protruding from its back, skin cracked with frost, and a long, protruding tongue dripping with frozen black liquid onto the snow.
Its eyes were red, grim, hungry.
And there were many similar shadows behind it.

[SCENE: CAVE BATTLE]
Reza didn't wait.
With a sharp shout, he struck the ground with his staff—blue flames bursting out blindingly.
The first creature burst into flames instantly, its silent scream sounding like the cracking of ice.
Dimas thrust his spear, slicing one creature apart, causing it to crumble into black shards.

They moved quickly, covering each other's backs, attacking without leaving any opening.
They were winning easily.

But every time one creature fell… two more emerged from outside the cave.
Their numbers continued to grow, creeping in like a black wave.

Reza gritted his teeth.
'Too many.' We're running!'

Dimas slashed another, and then they ran out of the cave, breaking through the biting blizzard.
The creatures gave chase, their feet crushing the snow silently.

[SCENE: SURFACE FINDING]
They were almost out of breath when—
a burst of light flashed in the distance.
Snow flew, the creatures' bodies flying.

A group of people—survivors—were fighting against the horde of black creatures.
Without thinking, Reza and Dimas jumped into the fray.

[SCENE: HUGE BATTLE]
Blue flames clashed with ice spears.
The survivors' swords glowed a pale blue, cutting down the creatures one by one.
The fight dragged on, black blood congealing into the white snow.

Finally…
As the orange glow of dusk appeared behind the clouds…
The creatures slowly retreated, dragging their bodies into a crevice in the rock.
They dispersed as night fell—when the temperature dropped to a lethal level.

[SCENE: SURVIVOR INTRODUCTION]
“You guys are amazing… it’s rare for anyone to survive this far,” said one of the survivors, a bearded man with sharp eyes.
They were friendly.
They offered dry food, furs, and a hot, bitter drink that stung their throats.

Conversation ensued throughout the short walk to their shelter:
a ruined stone building atop a snowy hill, surrounded by waist-high broken walls.
“We’ve survived here… six years,” they said quietly.

A night storm raged, the wind howling, swallowing the world.
Behind the crumbling walls, a small fire burned.
The survivor explained that there was a large settlement not far from here, at the foot of the icy valley, where dozens of people had gathered.

[SCENE: SUSPICIOUS NIGHT]
Reza and Dimas remained vigilant, sleeping in shifts.
But by morning, the survivors showed no signs of harm.
They seemed genuinely good people.

[SCENE: FAREWELL]
The exchange was made: a few small blue mana stones were exchanged for dried food, warm clothing, and a rough map to the settlement.
With the biting morning wind, Reza and Dimas descended the hills—toward the ice settlement the survivors had mentioned.
