[SCENE: DECEIVING SHADOW]
The giant shadow approached…
Reza and Dimas were ready to fight—

But as the figure stepped into the firelight… they froze.
The creature… was small.
No bigger than a rabbit, its fur gleaming white like new snow, its large, round eyes reflecting the light of their blue flames.

It turned out the illusion of the fire's shadow made it appear gigantic.
The tiny creature merely sniffed the ground, then quickly snatched a small, winged insect that approached the fire.
No fangs. No roar.
Just a small, crisp sound and innocent satisfaction.

Reza and Dimas looked at each other… then laughed softly—their first laugh on this floor.
The tension in their chests slowly released.

[SCENE: A SHORT REST]
The blue fire was still burning. They leaned against the cave wall, letting their bodies slowly adjust to the blood-chilling arctic air. Their breaths no longer formed a heavy mist. Their heartbeats returned to normal.

Hours passed.
The blizzard still roared outside, but no sound penetrated the thick stone walls.
In the silence, they began exploring the cave.

[SCENE: CAVE EXPLORATION]
The cave wasn't deep—just a large cavity that seemed to have been formed by thousands of years of erosion. The walls were smooth, the floor hard.
There were tiny, faintly glowing mushrooms, colonies of translucent insects, and… only one creature here: the tiny creature from earlier.

And strangely, the creature now followed them everywhere, walking tiny, on its round feet.
Sometimes it would stop, point to a particular mushroom with its nose, or scratch the ground to reveal a hidden insect.

Dimas whispered softly,
"Maybe it knows which ones are edible…"

Reza nodded softly.

[SCENE: NIGHT FALLS]
It was getting dark, and the storm outside was raging.
Snow blocked almost the entire cave entrance. They blocked the rest with large rocks, leaving only a small gap for air to pass through.
The heat from their small fire was now trapped inside, slowly melting the frost.

The small creature curled up like a cotton ball, fast asleep.
Reza and Dimas made a simple tent from scrap fabric and the bones of their folding tent.
The cold made their bodies feel heavy, and their eyelids closed by themselves.
In the warm, fragile embrace, they finally fell asleep.

[SCENE: DAYTIME]
A small roar broke the silence.
Reza stirred, Dimas groaned.
They slowly opened their eyes—bright, blinding white light.
The storm… had subsided.

But the small creature stood upright, barking softly at the cave entrance.
There was a shadow outside…

The stone covering the cave entrance was shifting.
There was someone out there.
No, it was a group of people.
Trying to open their way in.

Reza and Dimas stared at each other.
Their hands slowly reached for their weapons.
Their muscles tensed. Their breathing was deep.
They knew:

The survivors in this world…
…don't always come as friends.

They are getting ready.
