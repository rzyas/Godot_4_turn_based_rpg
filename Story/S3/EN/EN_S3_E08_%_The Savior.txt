A blizzard danced madly across the white field.
Dimas and Reza stood frozen behind a boulder, their bodies stiff, their fingers barely able to grip their weapons.

The footsteps grew closer.

They weren't just walking in the storm…
…they were pushing through it, as if the snow was pushing away from their bodies in fear.

The aura of magic surrounding the black-robed figures was so dense…
that even from tens of meters away, Reza's chest felt like it had been struck by an ice hammer.
Their instincts screamed—run. Escape. Don't fight.

But their legs only trembled.

Reza's breath froze in the air, breaking into mist.
Dimas closed his eyes, his lips trembling:
“…so this is the end, huh?”

[SCENE: A VERY FAMILIAR VOICE]

The storm roared, but the voice cut through everything.

“WOW, KID!
Long time no see!”

Dimas opened his eyes slowly.
Reza turned… confused.
That… was a familiar voice.

The silhouettes were still obscured by the storm, but suddenly—
“SWOOOSH!”

Two figures appeared behind them with teleportation magic, sharp blades gleaming at their necks.

They froze.
Unable to speak.
Unable to move.

[SCENE: SURPRISE EXPLOSION]

A few seconds of silence.

Then one of them—a tall, burly man in a black robe with blue trim—burst into laughter.

“Hahaha! Crazy… your faces…!
Relax, relax. We were—your saviors back on the second floor, remember?!”

Dimas and Reza blinked.

Reza nearly dropped his staff.

“YOU??!!”

The leader of the group nodded proudly.
“Yep. We heard rumors that two crazy kids had managed to kill the Frost Titan…
We thought… ah, it couldn’t be you. But it turns out… who else could be this ridiculous if not Reza and Dimas?”

The storm shook their small tent, but the tension melted away. The atmosphere changed drastically in an instant.

From Despair to Jokes

Several members of the mysterious group immediately sat in a circle by the fire, warming their hands.

One of them looked at the sumptuous basket of food Reza & Dimas had bought—smoked cheese, warm bread, salted meats.
He looked at them… and said flatly:

“We’re… hungry.”

Dimas raised his hands in resignation.
“Just take it.”

A few seconds later, laughter broke out.
Bread was tossed, cheese was fought over, and everything turned into a heated commotion.

Reza shook his head, holding back a smile.

“Just an hour ago I thought we were going to die… now they’re robbing us of our food while we laugh.”
[SCENE: SECRET OF THE MAGIC CITY]

Amidst the jokes and mouths full of food, the group leader pulled a round, gleaming mirror from an inner pocket of his robe.

On its surface, a reflection of the two of them… appeared from a distance.

“We came because… of this.”
He pointed to the mirror.
“You’re being watched. Someone has placed a magic eye on you.
We interrupted the signal as we approached… but they know you’re alive. And rich.”

The campfire crackled.
The laughter slowly died down.
Serious looks filled the air again.

Dimas swallowed hard.
Reza clenched his fists.

They weren’t alone in this snowy world.
And now… some enemy, who knows who, knew they were still alive.
