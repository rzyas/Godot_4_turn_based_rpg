The sky on the 3rd Floor split open.
A blizzard raged wildly, as if the world were screaming in terror.

Amidst shards of ice spinning like blades, Reza and Dimas stood… facing the Legendary Ice Dragon.

The winged giant hovered like an ancient god, its wings blocking out the world's light. Its breath froze the air, causing the ground to crack like glass.

Then…
war broke out.

[SCENE: A BATTLE THAT RAVES THE SKY]

A dazzling explosion of magic.
Reza ran over the cracked ice, his golden sword slashing through the air in an arc of blue flame.
Dimas lunged from the side, his black spear striking the dragon's wing with a loud bang.

But their attacks were almost ineffective.
Every scale of the dragon reflected a steel-like light, and every breath sent ice spiraling through the air.

Suddenly, the sky cracked—
the dragon opened a rift into its own personal dimension.
The vortex of light formed a dark world, filled with shards of ice floating like dead islands.

And there…

…thousands of skulls lay scattered.

Skulls of humans, giants, even other strange creatures—frozen, shattered, frozen again.
Reza froze in place when he saw one wearing broken armor covered in blue crystal.

“That…” he whispered softly.

Dimas turned—and his heart froze.
All the survivors who had once helped them… were now just fragile skeletons in the sea of ​​ice.

There was no trace of their laughter. No trace of their hope.
Only death… silent.

[SCENE: DEATH]

Reza roared, his magical aura exploding.
His golden sword blazed like a sun in a world of ice, piercing the dragon’s heart.

The dragon roared—a sound that shattered the continent of ice.
In desperation, the dragon swiped at Reza with its massive claws, shattering the ice island where he stood.

Time slowed.
Dimas tried to jump…
But Reza only stared at him—and smiled faintly.

“Go on… climb as high as you can…”

Then Reza’s body vanished, swallowed by an explosion of snow and light.
No body remained.
Only his sword fell, frozen… and shattered into light dust.

[SCENE: DIMAS’S FINAL WOUND]

Dimas roared so loudly that the air trembled.
His cries turned to power.

He thrust his spear into the icy ground, absorbing all the remaining black energy.
His body heated up, igniting a magical storm.

He leaped toward the dragon’s head and pierced its eye with a blast of blazing black energy.

The dragon screamed—its wings burned, its scales shattered, and its gigantic body collapsed to the ground.

But as the final explosion struck, the dragon’s claws struck Dimas…
ripping his right arm off.

His blood froze in the air like shards of red glass.
He fell to his knees, half frozen, half bleeding.

[SCENE: PORTAL OPENS]

The dragon thrashed one last time… then silence.
From its shattered chest, a giant heart of ice began to open like a flower.

And from within it…
a swirling black portal formed.

The wind from the portal sucked in all the ice, bones, and blood around it.
Dimas tried to hold on…
but his shattered body could no longer resist.

“Reza… I will continue…”

He was sucked into the black light.
Vanished.

The blizzard stopped.
The valley was silent, filled with ice, bones, and stillness…

Reza had fallen.
Dimas was mortally wounded and vanished.
The Legendary Ice Dragon had fallen.

And from the dragon’s heart… a new world awaited.
