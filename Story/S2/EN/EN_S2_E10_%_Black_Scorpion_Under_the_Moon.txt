The sky began to redden. The desert sun slowly set, leaving a dim orange glow. The night wind blew dry, bringing a bitter taste that bit at the skin.

Reza and Dimas stood weakly, surrounded by greedy survivors. Their lustful gazes were fixed on the giant blue crystal they had just obtained.

“Hand over the crystal, boys. You’re too weak to have it.”

A man wrapped in black cloth stepped forward, his voice cold, his eyes filled with arrogance.

Reza gripped his sword tightly, though his hands trembled. “We found it. It’s… ours!”

Dimas held his breath, pointing his spear at the ground. “If you want to take it, fight first!”

Laughter erupted from the survivors.
“Hahaha, you’re almost knocked down, and you dare to fight us?”

A fight broke out instantly.
Reza slashed quickly, but his attack was easily parried. Dimas tried to stab, but was kicked hard, sending his body crashing into the sand.
Their bodies were covered in wounds, their energy drained.

Night fell. The sky darkened, the moon began to rise. Both of them were gasping for breath, blood dripping onto the hot sand, which was now starting to cool.

“Dimas… I can’t take it anymore.”
“Me too, Reza… but don’t let them take this crystal.”

They tried to escape, stumbling while dragging the blue crystal. But their path was quickly blocked. The survivors stood before them, weapons drawn.

“You’re just stupid kids. Let us take advantage of it.”

Reza and Dimas fell, unable to fight anymore. Their vision began to blur. Their breaths were almost gone.

Then the earth shook.
BANG… BANG… BANG…
The ground shook violently, sand flying. From the darkness behind them, a terrifying sound could be heard—the sound of footsteps and the scraping of giant claws.

The survivors froze.
“What the hell is that…?”

From the darkness emerged a giant figure. A black scorpion the size of a building. Its pincers glinted darkly, its eyes glowing red, its curved tail ready to strike.

Reza and Dimas could only stare in resignation.
“Looks like… this is the end for us, Dimas.”
“Hahaha… yes, Reza. At least… we’ll see Karin again.”

Both of them laughed weakly, a bitter laugh as they approached death.

But what happened was different.
The giant scorpion didn’t attack. It swung its massive pincers, and with a thud, it hit the ground beside them, covering Reza and Dimas. Their bodies seemed to be protected, not attacked.

The greedy survivors took a few steps back, their faces pale.
“N-no way… why is that monster protecting them?!”

As fear gripped them, another sound echoed.

TSSSHHH—!
A shot of magic shot from the darkness.
SWOOOSH—!
The swing of a sword sliced ​​through the night.

Screams rang out.
One by one, the greedy survivors fell, their bodies covered in blood. Only one managed to escape, darting in panic into the desert night. The rest were slaughtered mercilessly.

Figures emerged from the shadows.
The group of hunters who had previously helped Reza and Dimas—their gazes were sharp, but now tinged with relief.

The giant scorpion remained standing behind, its pincers still protecting the two children, as if acknowledging their presence.

Reza, his vision growing dim, whispered softly.
"They... helped us again..."

Dimas smiled weakly, then his eyes closed.
Their bodies finally collapsed, unconscious.

All that remained were the screams of the greedy survivors, slowly fading into the night.
