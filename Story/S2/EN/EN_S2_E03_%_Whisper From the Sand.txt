Desperate screams echoed in the dark room.
The air was stuffy, mixed with the stench of dried blood and rotting flesh. Between the screams, the growls of waiting monsters could be heard, hungry and ferocious.

Reza and Dimas opened their eyes. Their vision was blurry. Their bodies were bound in magical chains, unable to move. Their skin burned, old wounds still throbbed, their bodies shattered from the previous battle.

Before them stood several figures in black robes. Their faces were covered by strange masks, and in their hands were magic wands pulsing with dark red light. They were performing a ritual—draining the life from the captives also bound around them.

One by one, the captives' bodies withered, their skin clinging to their bones, their eyes rolled back, and they died without another chance to scream.
Reza and Dimas could only stare, unable to help, waiting their turn.

It was silent. It was tense.
Only the sound of desperate sighs could be heard.

In the darkness of their hearts, something stirred. The shadowy figure that had been clinging to their souls began to struggle, trying to break free. But not to help. Not to give strength.

As if he knew their turn had come.

A black-robed wizard approached. His hand raised, chanting a spell in a foreign language. His wand glowed blood red.

And then—
Pain struck.

Reza and Dimas' bodies shook violently.
Their screams erupted.

“AAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHH!!”

The pain wasn't like any ordinary wound. It felt like their entire bodies were burning from within, as if their blood were boiling and their souls were being slashed repeatedly. They could feel something being forcibly pulled out: the dark power that had been dwelling within them.

Their bodies grew weaker. Muscles tensed, bones felt like they were breaking. They struggled, but the chains of magic tightened.

Tears fell without them realizing it.
The pain wasn't just physical—it was torture to the soul.

“Is… this… our end?” Reza's voice was hoarse, almost breaking.
“No… I… haven't…” Dimas tried to speak, but his voice broke, drowned out by screams.

Then—darkness.

Their bodies slumped.
They were barely breathing.
They weren't dead, but more like empty shells.

The sorcerer performing the ritual stared at them in surprise.
"Still alive...?"
Several other sorcerers turned their heads. They whispered to each other, surprised. But in an instant, the expressions vanished. There was no concern.

"It's no use. They're just failed vessels."

Reza and Dimas' bodies were thrown like trash, along with a pile of drained corpses. They fell into the sandy abyss outside the ritual fortress—an abyss filled with carrion-eating desert monsters.

Their bodies lay atop the other corpses, waiting their turn to be torn apart by the monsters.
The footsteps of the beasts approached. The growls of hunger grew louder.

But suddenly, a light appeared.

Not sunlight, not fire, but a warm, pure, golden light enveloped their bodies. Like a soft mist covering a wound, the light slowly pulled their bodies into the sand, deeper, out of the monsters' reach.

And amidst the darkness of the sand, a faint whisper reached their ears:
“Stay alive…”

When consciousness returned, Reza and Dimas gasped. They emerged from the sand, coughing, their bodies trembling. Around them, it was no longer the abyss of death, but the tents of a hunter's group.

The people were shocked to see two children emerge from the ground. Guns were immediately drawn, but one of the wizards from the camp approached, raising his wand, and a blue light shone on their faces.

“Humans… they’re humans!” he exclaimed.

The atmosphere changed. Weapons were lowered.
Reza and Dimas were immediately pulled into line, given water and medicine. Their bodies were still weak, but at least they were no longer in enemy hands.

In the distance, a majestic fortress loomed. Sturdy, standing amidst the harsh desert.
The royal fortress—the only safe haven for the survivors on the second floor.

They had survived… for now.

But who knows for how long.
