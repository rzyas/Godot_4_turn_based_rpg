It was dark.
Only the sound of labored breathing could be heard amidst the cold night and the suffocating stench of blood.

Karin lay helpless, her body pierced by the horns of a giant wolf. Her tiny eyes slowly lost their light, as if the world had simply ripped everything away.

Reza and Dimas were frozen. The two children were unable to accept the reality that had so cruelly exposed them. Their hearts were beating fast, but their bodies felt as if they had turned to stone.

'No... this isn't real... Karin...'

But the blood dripping to the ground, warm and red, told them otherwise.

The dying figure of the horned wolf was still moving. Though its body was torn and near death, the monster let out a final scream of rage. In that scream, its horns pierced Karin deeper until the little girl's body was completely destroyed.

Silence.

For a moment, the world seemed to stop.

All that remained was a name.

Karin's golden sword slipped from her grasp, fell to the ground, and slowly faded. The once-glowing weapon had now lost its master. Only faint shards of light swirled before vanishing into thin air.

Reza's eyes widened, frozen.
Dimas's eyes trembled, cracking.

Then, from the depths of their souls, something exploded.

The shadow that had been following them, the parasite that had once merely been a freeloader, was no longer separate. It merged completely, mingling with blood and rage, igniting an unstoppable fire of hatred.

From within Reza's body, an eruption of fire—WHOOSH!—was heard, as tongues of blue flame enveloped his body. The fire wasn't just hot, but searing through the soul, consuming the last vestiges of fear until only one word remained: kill.

Meanwhile, a pitch-black aura emanated from Dimas's body. Dark horns slowly grew from his head, piercing the night sky with brutal energy. The ground beneath his feet trembled, as if the very earth itself trembled at his anger.

Their screams tore through the air:
"AAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHH!!!"

Their screams weren't just pain, but revenge transformed into power.

A larger, monstrous figure emerged from the darkness. His red eyes blazed with hatred. His body wasn't just a wolf—it was a shadow transformed into a creature more savage, more vicious, and far more dangerous.

With a single thrust, he threw Reza and Dimas backward. Their bodies slammed into the ancient stone ruins, making a loud bang. But this time, they didn't fall. They stood back up. Their bodies were no longer those of mere children—they were inheritors of the power of darkness and blue fire.

Reza hissed, his voice hoarse with fire:
“You will die here…”

Dimas gripped his spear, now enveloped in a pitch-black aura.
“In the name of Karin… I will destroy you.”

And the battle erupted.

A giant gorilla monster emerged from the shadows, a summons from the boss monster. It leaped with fists as big as rocks, trying to crush the two boys.

But Reza stepped forward. Blue flames enveloped his hands, and with a single swing, it sliced ​​through the air. The first gorilla's body exploded, burning to ash.

Dimas refused to be defeated. His black spear thrust forward. With a single thrust, the ground cracked, a dark aura exploded, and three gorillas were sent flying, their chests smoking black holes.

Blood spurted in all directions.
The air was filled with the smell of burning flesh and torn earth.

The boss monster roared, plunging headlong at unnatural speed. Its giant claws nearly ripped Reza's head off, but this time Reza blocked it with both flaming hands. Blue flames locked onto the monster, searing its flesh, making it howl in pain.

On the other side, Dimas slammed his spear into the ground. A black wave spread, creating pillars of shadow that entangled the gorillas' legs. In an instant, he leaped into the air and plunged his spear into the head of one of the gorillas, causing it to tremble violently.

Black blood splashed down his face. But his eyes were blank—all that remained was revenge.

The fight grew increasingly brutal. Each attack was not merely a matter of survival, but an outlet for the burning sense of loss.

The shadow wolf attacked, but Reza countered with a fist of blue flame that blasted the air. Dimas followed, stabbing with a black aura that shook the earth.

The ground collapsed. Trees snapped. The air filled with the screams of monsters.

But from a distance, Karin's mangled body lay still. As if a silent witness to the rise of a new monster—not just an enemy, but also two children who were no longer the same.

Their eyes were empty, yet burning.
All that remained was revenge.
All that could be heard were whispers: "Kill... kill... kill..."

Blue flames blazed, black shadows expanded.
The battle grew more and more frenzied.

And just as Reza and Dimas were about to launch the final attack on the boss monster—

The world screen seemed to crack.
Darkness swallowed everything.
